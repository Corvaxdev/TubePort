# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TubePort contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, respectful, and safe. Constructive technical disagreement is
welcome; harassment and intimidation are not.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable, and respectful feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints;
- keeping discussions focused on the project and its users;
- protecting other people's privacy and security.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, insults, sexualized attention, or discriminatory
  language;
- sustained disruption, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussion;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring anyone to share credentials, cookies, private media, or account
  access;
- encouraging copyright infringement or bypass of access controls;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy. Enforcement should be proportionate, consistent,
and attentive to the safety and privacy of people involved.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using a private
contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue. Reports
will be reviewed as confidentially as practical; information may be shared only
when necessary to investigate or protect participants.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Deliberately false or
malicious reports may themselves violate this policy.
